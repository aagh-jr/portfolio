# EvidenceHero: rehearsal notes

- **Slot:** the Binu hero figure (binu.astro, `cs-hero-figure`), 976px wide at 1440 and 350px at 390. Its height is free.
- **Why a separate variant from GradeHero:** GradeHero is a 2x2 tile with a fixed height (660/720) and mostly illustrative content (pores, collagen, hydrates, soothes). The hero is the page's lead image, so it uses the product's own fixture content: the Products/EvidenceByClaim **Default** story, taken word for word from `claims.fixtures.ts`. Its height comes from measuring the table, so the card's foot always runs about 40px off the stage, whatever the wrapping. It shares the row and table styles of `evidence-grade.css` and doesn't change that file.
- **Reference:** `apps/web/src/components/products/EvidenceByClaim.tsx` and `.stories.tsx`, `fixtures/claims.fixtures.ts`, `packages/core/src/research/grading.ts` (CERTAINTY_META), `claim-badges.ts` (labels and cardText), `format/ingredient-name.ts`, `app/globals.css` and `app/layout.tsx` (font-mono = Space Grotesk). Screenshots of the stories `products-evidencebyclaim--default` (1440 and 1040), `--all-tiers` and `--mobile` (390).
- **Changes from the gold (GradeHero):** fixture data; stage height measured from the table; the last row gets 64px of bottom padding so the crop lands in padding; 24px radius to match the hero slot; Space Grotesk (the variable `public/previews/binu/space-grotesk-latin.woff2`, declared in evidence-grade.css) so the letter badges render as they do in the product, not in Courier.
- **Gate:** the one never-intentional line left is `spills its slot` on the ingredient chips. It's a false positive, the same one GradeHero has: the 220px column wraps Zinc PCA onto a second line, and the product's Storybook does the same. `near aligned` on the grade labels is the product's centered column.
- **Not checked:** dark mode (the case study is light only), reduced motion beyond the poster, and the widths between 760 and 976.
